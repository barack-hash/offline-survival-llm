Reconnect the remote, screen and Arduino

Seven jumper wires and one USB cable. The IR receiver and the LCD screen both plug into the Arduino, and the Arduino plugs into the Pi. The Arduino still has its program on it, so nothing needs flashing.

  • Arduino UNO
  • LCD1602 with I2C backpack
  • IR receiver (pins G R Y)
  • IR remote
  • 7 female-to-male jumper wires
  • USB-A to USB-B cable
  • Raspberry Pi 5 + its power supply
Keep everything unplugged while you wire. Pi power off, Arduino USB cable out. Female end of each jumper goes onto the module's pin, male end into the Arduino's socket.

    If something's off

    • Screen lights up but shows no textTurn the small blue screw on the back of the LCD slowly until letters appear. That's the contrast.
    • Screen stays darkCheck the LCD's VCC is on 5V and GND on GND. Swapped power wires are the usual cause.
    • Garbled or blank text after start-upSDA and SCL are probably swapped: SDA goes to A4, SCL to A5.
    • Remote does nothingThe receiver's small LED should blink on every press. If not: pull the clear plastic tab out of the remote's battery drawer, aim at the black dome from arm's length, and check the R wire is in IOREF.
    • Typing works but no answer comesGive the Pi a full minute after power-on to load. If it still says “Thinking…” forever, ask Claude to check the Pi's service.
